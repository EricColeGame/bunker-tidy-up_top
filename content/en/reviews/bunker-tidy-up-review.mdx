## Bunker Tidy Up Review: Gameplay and the Sorting Loop

The moment-to-moment experience is refreshingly simple. You walk through a bunker in first person, pick up supplies, and place them where they logically belong. There is no countdown clock, no threat, and no fail state waiting to punish a wrong decision. You can literally stand in a storage room for ten minutes deciding whether batteries belong with the radios or the tools.

What elevates the loop above pure busywork is item logic. Each of the 100+ item types has its own placement rules, so shelves are not interchangeable bins. Medicine next to the beds makes sense; medicine next to fuel does not. Learning those rules is the actual progression system, and it is where the game's gentle puzzle layer lives.

The first post-launch patch, released September 28, 2026, strengthened in-game skills and fixed several bugs based on player feedback — a good sign that the developers are actively tuning the experience rather than shipping and walking away.
