## How the Bunker Tidy Up Progression System Is Layered

Bunker Tidy Up doesn't hand you a skill tree on your first shelf. Progression is layered instead: you sort items, the game counts your placements, and those counts trip achievements that feed directly into the unlock system.

Community achievement tracking data makes this curve visible. The very first placement is earned by essentially every tracked player, while the final sorting milestones sit right around 40%. That spread is the entire progression arc in miniature.

Three layers matter:

- **Action layer** — placing, holding, and discarding items correctly.
- **Milestone layer** — achievements that fire at 100, 1,000, 5,000, and 10,000 correct placements.
- **Unlock layer** — skills, upgrades, and collectibles that make the action layer move faster.

Knowing which layer you're working on prevents the classic mistake of grinding placements while ignoring the unlocks that would have made those placements quicker.

## FAQ

**How many Bunker Tidy Up unlocks are there in total?**
The achievement set covers a skill unlock, a full skill tree completion, and a collectible sweep, alongside the sorting volume milestones. In practice, roughly 40% of tracked players finish both the item ceiling and the full skill upgrade, which suggests the two are closely linked.

**What's the fastest way to unlock skills?**
Place items correctly and consistently rather than quickly. The early milestones at 100 and 1,000 placements are where most skill points become available, so steady accuracy beats frantic discarding every time.

**Do collectibles affect Bunker Tidy Up unlocks?**
They're tracked separately, but skipping them keeps you below full completion. Community data puts collectible completion around 42%, close to the full skill mastery number.

**Why do most players stop around 40% completion?**
That's the point where volume milestones, the skill tree, and collectibles all converge. The final stretch demands sustained accuracy across thousands of placements, which is a very different task from the breezy first hour.

The short version: treat Bunker Tidy Up unlocks as a single system rather than three separate checklists. Sort accurately, upgrade whenever you can afford to, and sweep for collectibles as you go — and the 40% wall stops looking like a wall at all.
