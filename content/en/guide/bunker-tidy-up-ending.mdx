## The Three Phases of the Bunker Tidy Up Ending

Most players who actually reach the finish describe the same arc, and it breaks cleanly into three phases. Knowing them ahead of time helps you avoid the classic mid-game slump.

**Phase 1 — Bulk clearing.** You drag piles out of blocked rooms and triage them into rough clusters. Nothing is perfectly placed yet, and that's fine. The goal here is movement and visibility: get the debris off the floor so you can see what you're working with.

**Phase 2 — Category lockdown.** Once the bunker is breathing, you assign permanent homes: a food wall, a medical cabinet, a tool bench, a reading corner. Sorting by resource category is the intended approach, and it's what makes the later stages tolerable.

**Phase 3 — Final reconciliation.** This is the endgame, and it's where the Bunker Tidy Up ending is won or lost. The last few hundred stragglers — duplicate cans, orphaned tools, items you set down "for now" three hours ago — are the slowest items in the game.

## Tips to Reach the Bunker Tidy Up Ending Faster

Efficiency in a tidying sim comes from reducing decisions, not from moving faster. Every item you pick up twice is wasted time.

- **Set category rules before you touch anything.** Decide what "medical," "tools," and "pantry" mean in your bunker and never renegotiate.
- **Use entrance dump zones.** Pile everything from one room in one spot, then sort that pile once.
- **Reserve shelf space per category.** Overcrowded shelves force reshuffling later.
- **Unlock speed upgrades early.** Upgrades that let you move and place faster pay off across thousands of interactions.
- **Batch identical items.** Carrying five of the same item once beats carrying one item five times.
- **Leave the awkward items for last, but not too last.** Odd shapes and oversized gear need dedicated space.
- **Take breaks.** Sorting fatigue leads to sloppy placement, and sloppy placement means rework.

| Priority | Action | Why It Pays Off |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define categories up front | Eliminates repeat decisions |
| 2 | Build dump zones | Cuts walking distance dramatically |
| 3 | Unlock movement upgrades | Compounds across every item |
| 4 | Label shelves mentally | Speeds up late-game sweeps |
| 5 | Finish one room fully | Prevents cross-bunker backtracking |

## FAQ

**Is there more than one Bunker Tidy Up ending?**
Community reports point to a single completion state rather than multiple endings. The finish line is reached when every item is sorted, so there's no branching choice to worry about.

**Do I lose progress if I quit mid-sort?**
Since the game has no timer, you can stop whenever you like. Progress is tied to what you've placed, so leaving a messy room simply means returning to a messy room.

**How many items do I actually need to sort?**
Players report 17,437 total items across more than 100 unique supply types, which is why reaching the Bunker Tidy Up ending is a long-haul project rather than a weekend job.

**Is there a faster way to finish?**
Yes — build category rules early, create dump zones, and unlock movement upgrades first. Efficient decisions beat fast clicking every single time.

**Does the ending unlock anything special?**
No credible reports of secret content after completion. The reward is the fully organized bunker itself, plus the freedom to rearrange however you want.
