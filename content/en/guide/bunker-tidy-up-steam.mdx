## How to Sort 17,437 Supplies Without Burning Out

The biggest mistake new players make is trying to shelve items as they pick them up. That approach forces constant backtracking, and with thousands of objects scattered around a bunker, you will wear a path in the concrete before you finish a single room.

Instead, treat the bunker like a warehouse. Move first, organize second, and let the game's relaxed pacing work for you. Community reports consistently point to the same handful of habits that separate a smooth playthrough from a frustrating one.

### A Practical Three-Pass Workflow

| Pass | What You Do | Why It Helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Sweep | Walk the bunker and gather loose supplies into a central staging area | Cuts travel time dramatically and gives you a visual inventory |
| 2. Sort | Group the pile by category — food, medicine, tools, books | Large piles make patterns obvious that scattered items hide |
| 3. Shelve | Place each group onto its designated shelf in one trip | Fewer trips means fewer moments of "where did I leave that?" |

### Build a Landing Zone First

Pick one open stretch of floor near the middle of the bunker and treat it as your landing zone. Everything goes there before it goes on a shelf. In **Bunker Tidy Up**, the bunker layout rewards players who centralize early, because the walk between distant wings is where most of your time disappears.

### Fix Your Settings Before Your First Haul

Two small tweaks pay off immediately. First, adjust camera comfort and sensitivity so scanning shelves feels natural rather than twitchy. Second, set your volume mix so ambient sound stays present but never masks the audio cues tied to item placement. Both options exist in the accessibility menu, and both make long sessions far more comfortable.

### Stop Chasing Perfection

If you are the type who restarts a shelf because one label faces the wrong way, give yourself permission to move on. The game has no scoring penalty and no timer breathing down your neck. You can always return later — and you probably will, because tidying is strangely satisfying once the floor is clear.

## Supply Categories and Placement Logic at a Glance

Each item in **Bunker Tidy Up** follows its own placement logic. That is the puzzle layer hiding under the cleaning layer: a box of bandages does not belong where the canned peaches go, and the game quietly teaches you those rules as you handle more objects.

| Category | Typical Examples | Placement Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food | Cans, jars, dry goods, rations | Closest to sleeping and living areas |
| Medicine | Bandages, first aid kits, supplies | Easy-to-reach mid-height shelves |
| Tools | Hand tools, hardware, repair kits | Near workshop zones or utility rooms |
| Books | Manuals, paperbacks, reference texts | Anywhere dry and stable |
| Misc. | Decor, oddities, unlabeled crates | Fills gaps until better options appear |

A useful rule of thumb from player experience: sort by how often you would need an item in an emergency, then shelve the most critical categories at eye level. Heavy items go low, fragile items go where nothing can knock them over, and rarely used supplies can live in the far corners of the bunker.

| Heuristic | Best For | Watch Out For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sort by frequency of use | Medicine, tools | Shelves fill fast near the entrance |
| Sort by size and weight | Heavy crates, bulk goods | Fragile items can get crushed |
| Sort by color or label | Fast visual scanning | Breaks down when categories mix |
| Sort alphabetically | Books, manuals | Slow to set up at the start |

## FAQ About Bunker Tidy Up

**Is Bunker Tidy Up playable on Steam Deck?**
Yes. The game holds a Verified rating for Steam Deck, so it runs without manual tweaking. Smaller screen sizes do make fine label details harder to read, so many players prefer to sort broad categories on handheld and fine-tune the details on a desktop.

**Does Bunker Tidy Up have multiplayer or co-op?**
No. It is a single-player experience. There is no co-op mode, no competitive sorting, and no online component — the bunker is yours alone.

**How long does it take to sort all 17,437 supplies?**
There is no fixed answer because the game has no timers and lets you save at any moment. Pace depends entirely on how thorough you are. A player who sweeps, sorts, then shelves will move far faster than someone who places items one at a time as they explore.

**Is the launch discount still available?**
The introductory 20% discount was scheduled to end October 7. After that, the game returns to its standard price, though it may appear in seasonal Steam sales or as part of one of its many bundles.

**Does Bunker Tidy Up include Steam Achievements and Cloud saves?**
Yes to both. There are 14 achievements to unlock, and Steam Cloud support means your progress follows you between devices — handy if you like sorting a few shelves on a handheld and finishing the job at your desk.
