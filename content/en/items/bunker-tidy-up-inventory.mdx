Few things derail a relaxing sorting session faster than an overflowing bunker tidy up inventory. The moment your hands are full of mismatched supplies and half the shelves still sit empty, a calming organization game starts to feel like a second job. A clean bunker tidy up inventory isn't about raw speed — it's about matching every item to the correct shelf before you add more clutter to the pile.

The scale of the game is the real challenge. ## Core Sorting Rules for a Manageable Bunker Tidy Up Inventory

Everything in Bunker Tidy Up comes down to one rule: a shelf accepts an item only when the category matches. Place something incorrectly and it flashes red, refuses to count toward shelf progress, and quietly blocks completion until you move it. Treating that red flash as a warning sign rather than a minor annoyance is the difference between steady progress and hours of backtracking.

| Sorting Principle | Why It Matters | What to Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct category first | Misplaced items don't count toward shelf progress | Read the shelf label before unloading your hands |
| Bulky items early | Large supplies eat floor space and hide smaller objects | Clear generators, water containers, and armor first |
| Group identical items | Duplicates reduce the number of return trips | Carry one item type at a time |
| Clear visible piles | Buried supplies are easy to overlook | Remove surface clutter before deep searching |
| Sweep vertically | Items spawn upstairs, downstairs, and beside furniture | Finish one floor before changing levels |

### Start With the Bulky Supplies

Water containers, generators, body armor, backpacks, and fuel canisters are the easiest wins in the game. They are big, visually distinct, and usually grouped in open areas, so clearing them opens sight lines across the room. Once those disappear, the remaining clutter becomes far easier to read.

### Respect the Carrying Limit

Your starting carry capacity is deliberately small. Filling your hands with random objects means you'll arrive at the correct shelf holding the wrong things. Pick a target category, gather every matching object you can find, then make a single purposeful trip. Long-time players report that trip planning matters more than movement speed in the early hours.

## Upgrade Order That Speeds Up Every Bunker Tidy Up Inventory Trip

Upgrades decide how fast you gather, identify, and place supplies. The strongest early purchases reduce searching and walking rather than adding convenience. Magnet and Match Finder top that list because both directly shrink the time you spend hunting for the items your bunker tidy up inventory still needs.

| Upgrade | Main Function | Best Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magnet | Pulls nearby matching items into your hands | Clearing common supplies spread across large rooms |
| Match Finder | Highlights other items of the same type | Finding hidden or distant duplicates |
| Carry Capacity | Increases how many items you can hold | Cutting down on return trips |
| Quick Step | Raises movement speed | Crossing multi-floor layouts efficiently |
| Right Shelf | Shows where the held item belongs | Solving uncertain category calls |
| Auto Loader | Places or retrieves items while holding the button | Speeding up repeated shelf interactions |
| Scatter | Sends held items to shelves that already contain matches | Cleaning up large piles of identical objects |

A simple decision process keeps your upgrade points from going to waste:

1. Identify your bottleneck. Is it searching, carrying, walking, or placing?
2. Buy Magnet once you know which category you're completing and can see multiple copies nearby.
3. Add Match Finder right after picking up your first item, and memorize the highlights before they fade.
4. Invest in Carry Capacity only when your hands fill up before a route is finished.

Avoid buying abilities with long cooldowns simply because they look powerful. If an upgrade doesn't reduce your current bottleneck, save the points.

## Route Planning for a Cleaner Bunker Tidy Up Inventory

A reliable sweep pattern beats raw movement speed every time. The bunker spans multiple levels, with supplies tucked beside couches, behind banisters, and on raised platforms. Running the same loop in the same order makes it obvious when something has been missed.

| Route Phase | Target Supplies | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Water containers, generators | Sweep open upper and lower areas |
| Phase 2 | Armor, backpacks, fuel | Repeat the same path while the floor is clear |
| Phase 3 | Weapons and protection gear | Check corners, furniture edges, and platforms |
| Phase 4 | Medicine and cleaning products | Search cleared zones for small items |
| Phase 5 | Food, books, household goods | Finish by category using matching abilities |

| Search Problem | Likely Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One item still missing | Buried under clutter or behind furniture | Clear nearby piles and check floor edges |
| Shelf stuck at 90% | An item sits on the wrong category shelf | Read labels and move misplaced supplies |
| Room looks empty | Small items blend into the background | Trigger Match Finder or inspect clear piles |
| Routes feel endless | Too many category switches per trip | Finish one item group before moving on |

When Match Finder activates, pause for a beat. Scanning the whole room before the highlight expires can save several wasted trips. If a visible item seems unreachable, approach from a different angle instead of fighting the same path — furniture in this bunker blocks movement more than it appears to.

## Tricky Categories That Break Your Bunker Tidy Up Inventory Flow

Some shelves are self-explanatory; others demand a second look. Food is the worst offender, splitting into separate shelves for drinks, groceries, canned vegetables, canned meat and fish, preserved goods, sweets and snacks, spices and sauces, and animal food. Similar packaging does not mean the same category.

| Item Group | Common Examples | Sorting Reminder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survival equipment | Water containers, generators, backpacks | Visible early and fast to clear |
| Weapons and protection | Shotguns, axes, bats, armor, gas masks | Keep tools separate from medical supplies |
| Household goods | Toilet paper, toothpaste, cleaning products | Inspect labels when items look alike |
| Food and drinks | Soda, water, cereal, canned goods, jars | Use the exact food subcategory |
| Animal food | Cat food, dog food | Place only on the dedicated pet shelf |
| Books | Farming, healing, survival, hunting | Read the shelf description before unloading |

### Books Are Their Own Puzzle

Books cover healing, survival, farming, hunting, technology, repair, and bunker life. Until you memorize the shelf labels, treat each one as an individual sorting question rather than assuming a general "knowledge" shelf exists.

### Don't Forget the Small Stuff

Cleaning products and toiletries are easy to overlook because they're tiny compared to generators and weapons. Once the large objects are gone, make a dedicated household sweep instead of picking them up opportunistically. If an item turns red, don't force it — pick it up, read the neighboring labels, and try the most specific category available.

## Finishing Strong: The Last Items in Your Bunker Tidy Up Inventory

The final stretch always feels slower. What remains is small, hidden, or sitting behind furniture, and motivation tends to drop right when precision matters most. Use completed shelves as visual anchors and search only the zones that still hold loose objects.

| Endgame Task | Priority | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete nearly finished shelves | High | Few searches needed for quick progress |
| Use Magnet on common categories | High | Clears many matching items at once |
| Check behind furniture | High | Hidden supplies often remain there |
| Recheck red placements | Medium | Misplaced items block shelf completion |
| Hunt isolated small objects | Medium | Best left until major clutter is gone |

A finishing checklist that works well:

- Sort every visible water container and generator
- Complete armor, backpack, and fuel categories
- Clear remaining weapons and protection gear
- Separate food, drinks, medicine, and animal food
- Inspect buried objects and furniture edges before calling it done

Completing categories often awards upgrade points, so closing out several small shelves can be smarter than obsessing over one stubborn item.

## FAQ

**What is the fastest way to organize a bunker tidy up inventory?**

Start with large, easily spotted supplies such as water containers, generators, armor, backpacks, and fuel. Then finish one category at a time while leaning on Magnet and Match Finder to cut down on searching.

**Which upgrades should I buy first?**

Magnet and Match Finder are the strongest early picks because they improve both collection and discovery. Carry Capacity and Quick Step become more valuable once your routes get longer and your hands fill up faster.

**Why does an item turn red on a shelf?**

A red item is sitting on the wrong category shelf and won't count toward completion. Remove it, read the nearby labels, and place it in the most specific matching category.

**How do I find the last missing supply in Bunker Tidy Up?**

Clear surrounding clutter, check behind furniture and beneath visible piles, then sweep each floor using the same route you always use. Match Finder helps most when a single item type is one copy short of completion.

The most dependable approach is orderly rather than rushed: clear the big items, upgrade your collection tools, finish categories in focused batches, and save the final sweep for whatever is hiding in the corners.
